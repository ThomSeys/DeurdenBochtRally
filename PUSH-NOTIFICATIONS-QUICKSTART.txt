3 Tabs:

  2. BROADCAST  - Send to everyone
  3. TARGETED   - Send to specific participants


KEY CONCEPTS
========================================

Event Type:     Category of notification
                Examples: zone_opened, critical_event

Target Type:    Who receives it
                Options: broadcast, targeted, single

History:        Complete log of all sent messages
                Includes success/failure counts

Delivery Log:   Per-recipient tracking
                Shows who got it, errors, etc.

Retry:          Resend failed messages
                One-click from admin dashboard
